WELCOME BACK EVERYBODY, TO THE FAMED KOZMOLOTTO. It is I, your host, KozmoFox! And its been so long since I've run the lotto, half of you are probably new and have no goddamn idea what it is, but allow me to toot my own ego for a moment and you will quickly understand.

Several of my "wetting experiences", as they were, make up a good amount of the very top posts in the sites history, including KozmoLotto 4, which is the top rated post in omorashi.org history at almost 100 rep points. This is where the ego-tooting comes in: I'm very, very good at what I do. This is the incentive to participate in my lotto. Why do I do this lotto? Because on one hand I like putting out content about my experiences for everyone to enjoy with my fabled authors mind, but on the other hand, a normal person doesn't have accidents to write about every single day. So I had to make more happen. Sure this could be as simple as drinking a ton of water and going for a drive, but I decided to involve the community. This is where the Kozmo-Lotto comes in.

What is the Kozmo-Lotto? It is essentially a lottery where people compete in a pseudo game of chance to decide how my next accident will happen. A member of the community can roll some virtual dice, and they will get a number. With their number, they will post a scenario or challenge. An example scenario being "Drink water until you're at a 7/10. Go to an arcade. Try to play every single game there once without peeing your pants in public. Come back and write about it." Of course this can be as in depth as a member wants, with some members going above and beyond, such as Rainyday who came up with an entire series of challenges over the course of the evening, that were delivered to me one by one via email so I wouldn't know the challenges in advance. That was Kozmo Lotto 4, you should check it out.

At the end of a period of time, which is usually when I notice participation beginning to die down, at which point I roll my own number. Whoever's number is closest to my number wins the lottery, and has their scenario fulfilled. I will complete this scenario, come back, and write one of my ever-beloved experiences about it. Simple, eh? Its an omorashi lottery!

How do I participate, you ask? Also simple!

Go here: https://www.wizards.com/d20modern/d20mdice/dice.htm

Roll the d100. This will roll you a number between 1 and 100. Screencap your number, and post the screencap in your reply, as well as your scenario. These things are mandatory. And thats it! If you win at the end of the lotto period, I will make a post tagging you so you know! Only one roll of the die per person!

Too many people come in and just roll on the off chance they win, and disappear forever without a word and forget this ever existed. If you're going to participate I want you to be at least a little invested in the result, you know? If you really honest to god can't think of something, say so, and come back and post it when you do.
