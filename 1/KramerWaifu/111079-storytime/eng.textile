Storytime

Author: KramerWaifu

*000*2025-04-07 21:29

So this happened a couple years ago, was up in the city with a friend which was about an hour or so from home. We were up in chinatown all day and decided to have dinner before we left. Had 4 or 5 beers at dinner with the generous portion of orange chicken. Anyway we get to driving and it hits me that I gotta GO. So since we're leaving the city around 4 I say to myself, "Oh I'll just hold it until we get out of the city so I don't have to try to park again."

Big mistake

We get on the expressway and about a mile in BAM deadlock traffic. Should have known with it being rush hour out of the city. We FINALLY get to an exit 15 minutes later that looks like somewhere will have a bathroom. First stop: Gas station and NO bathroom.

Okay, I'll drive down a ways and see if there's anything else.

Every. Single. Business.

Had a policy of employees only. (Mind you, none of them were gas stations or restaurants)

I had driven down from the highway about 10 minutes. At this point I can feel myself swelling. The pains are really setting in. And when I get back into my car it instantly hurts to sit down.

My friend can obviously tell it's killing me.

He turns to me and goes, "Dude you've had to go for like 30 minutes. Just go in an alley."

I can't. It's broad daylight and I tell myself, nah I'll just get back on the highway and hit somewhere at the next exit.

Now this is where it will sound unbelievable.

At the next exit which was 2 miles away I was wiggling in my seat the entire time and what do I see? There was an accident ON THE RAMP and I couldn't take it.

The annoyance, disappointment, and pure pain in my abdomen almost had me screaming.

The NEXT exit I took had a gas station to the left and to the right a target. I figure, okay I'll stop at the gas station. Big mistake. Out of service.

I could not take it anymore. I had been complaining this whole time to my friend about how, "Dude I'm not gonna make it" "I have never had to piss so bad in my life"

I legit hadn't taken my hand off my crotch for the better part of 45 minutes at this point. I was starting to actually get red in the face.

I come back to the car and my friend goes, "Feel better?" I don't even respond. I just slam it in drive and go as fast as I can to the Target.

I don't even park it in a spot. I leave it up front of the store and literally run in.

The deluge leaving me was nothing like I had ever experienced before or since and I hope to never again.

When I finally get back to the car my friend goes, "So did you actually piss yourself or what?"

"Nah man. I'm all good"
