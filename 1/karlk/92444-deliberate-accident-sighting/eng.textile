This happen some years ago when I was around 20 years old and I was still living in my home town. I was out for a walk time must have been around 4pm. My house was quite close to a high school so I saw that some of the student on their way home. I recognized one of the girls since we went to the same school when we were younger, and her mum was a hairdresser and used to cut my hair (it was a very small town). She must have been 17-18 years old, blonde, maybe 170 cm, wearing light blue jeans and carrying a black laptop case in her arms. She said bye to her friends and started walking in another direction along the same path I was about to walk, but some 50 meters ahead of me. Her house was a 300 m walk from where we were and my walk usually passes by their house since that is the easiest way to reach the trails so I somehow ended up behind her. 

She didn’t seem to be desperate at all so I was mostly thinking about that awkward situation when you walk slightly faster than someone ahead of you so have to find a good way to overtake them. In order to reach her street you go down this short trail and you end up right at their house so when she finally reached the street I was some 10-15 meter behind and she didn’t seem to have noticed me. Just when she was 5 meters from the door, she looked up and down the street but forgot to look up the trail where I was.

Then she began to cross her legs and looking desperate, holding her crotch. I froze; I didn’t know what to do. I didn’t want to stop walking since if she would have seen me it would have been really creepy, so I continued walking. It was only me and her on the street. Then it happened; she spread her legs and you could see a dark spot growing on her butt. Then, when I was 3 meters from here she finally saw me and stood up (she had only peed for a couple of seconds) and turned around like nothing had happen. I continued walking and pretended that I was looking the other way and that I hadn’t realized what she was doing (I didn’t want to embarrass her) even though my head was rushing.

Unfortunately I couldn’t pass by their house later to see the puddle she made but I’m pretty sure she did it on purpose considering the sudden desperation and the quick stop of peeing when she saw me. That was also the last time I saw her unfortunately.
